Maganti was motivated by skepticism over Jarred Sumner's claim that Bun's new Rust build was 5x faster than the old Zig build. He reproduced the numbers (24m Zig vs 5m40s Rust on his own machine) and used buildprof to investigate. The analysis revealed: the Zig build's 16m linker step dominated, running Full LTO on the entire codebase at the end. The linker was then waiting for bun-zig.o to finish compilation.
