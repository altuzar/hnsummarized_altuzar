Core challenge: original matrix drive is too slow; cutting traces and soldering individual H-bridges per dot (Mike's approach) requires CNC and desoldering labor that becomes economically unfeasible. The first 13×8 prototype board suffered voltage sag when pulsing all dots simultaneously (~50A demand for 1ms pulses). Capacitor selection mattered: small 0805 ceramics in parallel underperformed versus larger single capacitors under voltage stress.
