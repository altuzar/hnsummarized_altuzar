    ★ Interactive visualizations of word-choice dice rolling, key coloring, and mark detection…

    Watermarks in AI-generated text work by biasing word selection toward secretly-colored choices without changing meaning. A secret key divides candidate words into green/red groups and tilts the model's dice slightly toward green; only the key-holder can recolor text and count if greens appear too often to be chance. Detection requires 1,500+ words for reliable flagging and fails completely with heavy rewriting that breaks original phrasing continuity.
