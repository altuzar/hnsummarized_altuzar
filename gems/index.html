  3. ★
    E-means: 10x faster k-means at equal loss(e-means.github.io)

    ★ Interactive playground with live visualization of e-means vs Lloyd on toy datasets, plus…

    e-means replaces Lloyd's k-means algorithm with exponential moving average centroid updates plus cosine-scheduled learning rates, dead-cluster revival via splitting, and automatic hyperparameter selection via scaling laws. Achieves 10-45x wall-clock speedup on GPU and 8-15x on CPU while reaching better final clustering loss in 1.2-3 epochs versus Lloyd's 25, with no hyperparameter tuning needed.
