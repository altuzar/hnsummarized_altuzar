He begins with outer-product memory (capacity d_k=64), analyzes interference patterns when querying random stored vectors, then progressively improves via: (1) optimal linear regression (capacity 2d_k), (2) ReLU MLPs treating nonlinearity as interference suppression (capacity 2d_k), (3) squared ReLU / GLU variants (capacity ~2d_k²/3 ≈ 2700), and (4) exponential weighting via softmax attention (capacity ~e^(2√d_k) ≈ 1.2M). Each step mathematically justifies architectural choices found in modern transformers. For long contexts, scaling β proportionally to log M (via YaRN, LogN-Scaling, or entropy-conservation rules) maintains recall quality indefinitely.
