The defense examples are concrete. Raytheon restarted Stinger production in 2023 by rehiring retired engineers in their 70s with 1970s paper blueprints; a 2022 order wasn't deliverable until 2026. Root causes weren't money but shattered supply chains: France shut propellant production in 2007 (17 years of nothing), Germany had two days of ammunition stockpile, and the single TNT facility was offline.
