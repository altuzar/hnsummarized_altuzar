Recursive generalization—where neurons sprout related sub-concepts as they grow—explains apparent polysemanticity. In retrained InceptionV1, moving from ~0% interpretable neurons in the last layer to 99-100% required only ordinary architectural improvements, no model scaling. In large language models like Qwen3-32B, preliminary audits find ~90% of neuron poles have one main job, ~7% have two related jobs, and only ~2-4% remain unexplained.

He emphasizes that interpretability compounds—like a jigsaw puzzle, each explained neuron constrains surrounding ones.
