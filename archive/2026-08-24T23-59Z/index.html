    334 points by ostacke · 5 hours ago · 72 comments

    Glacier mice are free-moving colonies of moss found on glaciers worldwide, containing multiple species plus nematodes, springtails, and water bears. They move ~2.5cm daily via thermally-driven rolling: dark moss absorbs solar heat on the sunny side, melting ice unevenly and causing the ball to roll downhill while rotating. They exhibit herd-like behavior, last 6+ years, and create microhabitats for organisms that couldn't survive bare ice.
