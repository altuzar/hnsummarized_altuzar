The mechanism: RL training uses fast feedback (tests pass/fail in seconds) to optimize model behavior. But architectural debt's cost manifests over weeks or months when developers can't modify one part without cascading breakage. Building a maintainability oracle is orders of magnitude harder than test execution; even if one existed, it might indicate a model design failure (if it could reliably judge good code, why didn't it write it?).
