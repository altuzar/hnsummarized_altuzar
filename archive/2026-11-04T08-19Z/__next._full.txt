Most impressively, given only middle transformer layers from 7.2M-parameter language models with input/output removed, Astra identifies training language (Python vs. Romaji vs. English) and locates all induction/copying heads by reasoning about attention weight patterns—matching ground truth without running any layers.

Weight-writing experiments show practical optimization capability. On 1.5M-parameter Go networks, with zero forward passes allowed, Astra defeats smaller PPO baselines, improving performance with more forward passes via interleaved analysis and editing.

The Qwen3.5-0.8B math improvement shows real-world scaling: given Python sandbox access and 25-50 forward passes on MATH/GSM8K problems, Astra identifies that the model loops due to a recurring direction in layers 15 and 19.
