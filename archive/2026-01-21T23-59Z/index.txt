The proposal allows entrepreneurs to register a company in any EU member state with one login, one central registry, and 48-hour fully digital incorporation. Crucially, tax and employment obligations follow real business activity (where employees work and operations occur), not the registration seat—preventing tax arbitrage and conflicts with national law. The structure includes standardized employee stock options (ESOs) with deferred taxation and safe-harbor valuation, addressing Europe's inability to compete with US tech firms on compensation. The linked page is primarily a campaign site coordinated by major VC firms (Atomico, Accel, Index Ventures, Seedcamp, EQT) and founded by Niklas Zennström and others, pushing for political support before final negotiations complete this fall. An October 2026 update notes the proposal has been "hollowed out"—the central registry was already cut from draft text, and stock options remain "under threat."
