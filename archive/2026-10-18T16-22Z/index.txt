For P-256, the published seed came from candidate iteration 1,842 of a run seeded 1997-08-16 01:52:34 UTC. The discovery involved pattern recognition (noticing all top bits were zero in binary-field seeds—probability 1/5810 by chance, a signature of lrand48's 31-bit output), then recovering the full 48-bit PRNG state using LLL lattice reduction on truncated outputs.

Claude also identified base-point generation for six curves: FreeLIP's multiplicative congruential generator seeded with small integers like 20000, 20420, etc., discarding seven outputs then generating x-coordinates until finding points on the curve. Eleven of sixteen seeds fully explained; four NIST base points and one B-163 seed remain unexplained.
