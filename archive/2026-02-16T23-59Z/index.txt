Real-world usage typically involves skills emerging iteratively from actual problem-solving, human refinement, or research augmentation.

Domain-level results reveal the most interesting insight: skills provide massive gains in healthcare (+51.9pp) and manufacturing (+41.9pp) but minimal gains in SWE (+4.5pp). Commenters correctly interpret this: skills matter most where models have weakest training priors — exactly the domains where procedural knowledge is proprietary, hard-won, and most valuable. In SWE, models already encode extensive training data from code repositories, so skills add little marginal value.
