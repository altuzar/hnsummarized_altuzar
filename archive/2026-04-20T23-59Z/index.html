    307 points by hasheddan · 7 hours ago · 109 comments

NIST, BSI, and crypto researchers agree 128-bit symmetric keys need no quantum-era upgrades; switching is wasteful churn diverting resources from urgent post-quantum asymmetric cryptography transitions.
