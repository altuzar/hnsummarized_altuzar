Initial experiments prove reading capability: Astra identifies which of four games trained identical small PPO networks by analyzing 17,253 weight parameters, correctly names all four games without options provided. Most impressively, given only middle transformer layers from 7.2M-parameter language models with input/output removed, Astra identifies training language (Python vs. Romaji vs. English) and locates all induction/copying heads by reasoning about attention weight patterns—matching ground truth without running any layers.

Weight-writing experiments show practical optimization capability. On a Kinetix car-ramp task where PPO converges to local minimum, Astra edits 3.5% of weights in a single attempt, transforming 0% success to 100% across 1,024 test episodes by inserting a small separate decision path. Zero-shot MinAtar game-playing networks generated by Astra beat PPO trained 100 million steps across three games, and reach 60% of 500M-step PPO performance. On 1.5M-parameter Go networks, with zero forward passes allowed, Astra defeats smaller PPO baselines, improving performance with more forward passes via interleaved analysis and editing.

The Qwen3.5-0.8B math improvement shows real-world scaling: given Python sandbox access and 25-50 forward passes on MATH/GSM8K problems, Astra identifies that the model loops due to a recurring direction in layers 15 and 19. The model card's prior warning about looping loops suggests this is a genuine structural issue.

One mixed result: identifying sycophantic behavior from weights alone failed; Astra needed attention pattern inspection on self-written prompts to distinguish between models, then correctly located the difference in middle-layer attention heads reweighting toward user suggestions.
