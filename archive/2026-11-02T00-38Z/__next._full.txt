The compiler recognizes offset patterns (e.g., cases 97-101) and adjusts accordingly. It handles contiguous blocks efficiently by reducing them to two boundary comparisons instead of individual checks, factoring those into the heuristic calculation.

For non-contiguous sparse cases, GCC emits bitset tests using the BT (bit test) instruction.
