Through MIR and IR analysis, the author identifies that even trivial async blocks like `async { 5 }` get compiled into massive state machines with Unresumed, Returned, and Panicked states, despite needing none. The bar across just two awaits expands from 23 to 360 lines of MIR.

Key issues identified: (1) The Returned state panics on further polls for safety, but this panic overhead could be replaced with Poll::Pending in release mode without violating Future contract semantics, saving 2-5% binary size.
